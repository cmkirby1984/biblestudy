# New Testament (27 Books)

The New Testament reveals Jesus Christ as the fulfillment of God's promises and tells the story of the early church.

---

## The Gospels - 4 Books
Four accounts of Jesus' life, ministry, death, and resurrection.

- [Matthew]({{ '/new-testament/01-matthew' | relative_url }}) - Jesus as the promised King and Messiah
- [Mark]({{ '/new-testament/02-mark' | relative_url }}) - Jesus as the suffering Servant
- [Luke]({{ '/new-testament/03-luke' | relative_url }}) - Jesus as the perfect Son of Man, Savior of all
- [John]({{ '/new-testament/04-john' | relative_url }}) - Jesus as the divine Son of God

---

## History - 1 Book
The birth and explosive growth of the early Christian church.

- [Acts]({{ '/new-testament/05-acts' | relative_url }}) - The Holy Spirit empowers the church to spread the gospel

---

## Paul's Letters - 13 Books
Theological teaching and practical instruction from the Apostle Paul.

### Letters to Churches
- [Romans]({{ '/new-testament/06-romans' | relative_url }}) - The gospel: justification by faith and transformed living
- [1 Corinthians]({{ '/new-testament/07-1-corinthians' | relative_url }}) - Correcting divisions and moral issues in the church
- [2 Corinthians]({{ '/new-testament/08-2-corinthians' | relative_url }}) - Paul's ministry and defense of apostleship
- [Galatians]({{ '/new-testament/09-galatians' | relative_url }}) - Freedom in Christ vs. legalism
- [Ephesians]({{ '/new-testament/10-ephesians' | relative_url }}) - Unity and identity in Christ
- [Philippians]({{ '/new-testament/11-philippians' | relative_url }}) - Joy and humility in Christ
- [Colossians]({{ '/new-testament/12-colossians' | relative_url }}) - The supremacy and sufficiency of Christ
- [1 Thessalonians]({{ '/new-testament/13-1-thessalonians' | relative_url }}) - Encouragement and Christ's return
- [2 Thessalonians]({{ '/new-testament/14-2-thessalonians' | relative_url }}) - Perseverance and the Day of the Lord

### Pastoral Epistles
- [1 Timothy]({{ '/new-testament/15-1-timothy' | relative_url }}) - Instructions for church leadership and sound doctrine
- [2 Timothy]({{ '/new-testament/16-2-timothy' | relative_url }}) - Paul's final words: endure and preach the word
- [Titus]({{ '/new-testament/17-titus' | relative_url }}) - Organizing the church and living godly lives

### Personal Letter
- [Philemon]({{ '/new-testament/18-philemon' | relative_url }}) - Forgiveness and reconciliation in Christ

---

## General Letters - 8 Books
Letters written to broader Christian audiences.

- [Hebrews]({{ '/new-testament/19-hebrews' | relative_url }}) - Christ is superior to all; don't turn back
- [James]({{ '/new-testament/20-james' | relative_url }}) - Faith that works: practical Christian living
- [1 Peter]({{ '/new-testament/21-1-peter' | relative_url }}) - Hope and holiness in suffering
- [2 Peter]({{ '/new-testament/22-2-peter' | relative_url }}) - Growing in grace and guarding against false teachers
- [1 John]({{ '/new-testament/23-1-john' | relative_url }}) - Assurance of salvation and walking in love
- [2 John]({{ '/new-testament/24-2-john' | relative_url }}) - Walking in truth and love
- [3 John]({{ '/new-testament/25-3-john' | relative_url }}) - Hospitality and supporting gospel workers
- [Jude]({{ '/new-testament/26-jude' | relative_url }}) - Contending for the faith against false teachers

---

## Prophecy - 1 Book
Revelation of the end times and Christ's ultimate victory.

- [Revelation]({{ '/new-testament/27-revelation' | relative_url }}) - Christ's return, judgment, and the new creation
