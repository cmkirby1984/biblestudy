# John

## Book Overview

**Author:** John, "the disciple whom Jesus loved," son of Zebedee
**Date of Writing:** Approximately 85-95 AD (last Gospel written)
**Historical Context:** Written after decades of reflection; addressing early heresies about Jesus
**Original Audience:** Both believers and seekers; universal audience
**Purpose:** "That you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name" (20:31)
**Genre:** Gospel; theological reflection on Jesus' identity

## Key Themes & Messages

### Major Themes

1. **Jesus as Divine Son of God**
   - Prologue: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was God"
   - Seven "I AM" statements claim divine identity
   - Unity with the Father emphasized
   - Explicit deity of Christ

2. **Belief and Eternal Life**
   - Central purpose: that readers may believe and have life
   - Faith as trust in and commitment to Jesus
   - Eternal life begins now, not just future
   - Belief results in transformation and relationship

3. **Signs and Glory**
   - Seven signs reveal Jesus' glory and identity
   - Miracles point to spiritual realities
   - Glory revealed supremely in crucifixion
   - Signs designed to produce faith

4. **Love**
   - God's love demonstrated in sending His Son
   - Jesus' love shown in washing feet and laying down life
   - Command to love one another as Jesus loved
   - Love as mark of true discipleship

5. **Light and Darkness**
   - Jesus as light of the world
   - Choice between light and darkness
   - Those who believe come to the light
   - Rejection of light brings judgment

### Central Message

John presents Jesus as the eternal Word, fully divine Son of God who became flesh to reveal the Father and give eternal life to all who believe. Through seven miraculous signs and extended teaching discourses, Jesus reveals His identity and mission. The Gospel emphasizes that eternal life comes through believing in Jesus as the Christ, the Son of God. Love defines both God's nature and the character of Jesus' followers. Written with profound theological depth, John calls readers to life-transforming faith in Jesus as God incarnate.

## Practical Application

**For Daily Living:**
- **Abide in Christ**: Like a branch connected to the vine, maintain constant connection with Jesus through prayer, Scripture, and obedience (John 15)
- **Love One Another**: Make Christ's sacrificial love the pattern for your relationships—serve, forgive, and lay down your life for others
- **Walk in the Light**: Choose truth and transparency over hiding in darkness; confess sin and live openly before God and trusted believers
- **Believe and Receive Life**: Recognize that eternal life isn't just future hope but present reality—live today in the confidence of your relationship with God
- **Bear Witness**: Like the blind man who simply shared what Jesus did for him, testify about how Christ has changed your life

**For Spiritual Growth:**
- Meditate on the seven "I AM" statements to deepen your understanding of Jesus' identity and sufficiency for every need
- Study Jesus' high priestly prayer (John 17) to understand His desires for your unity with Him and other believers
- Reflect on the Prologue (John 1:1-18) regularly to grasp the wonder of the Incarnation—God becoming flesh
- Practice believing without seeing, trusting Jesus' promise that those who believe without seeing are blessed (John 20:29)
