## How to Use These Tables

1. **Teaching Prep** - Print and use as handouts for your Bible study group or class
2. **Quick Review** - Refresh your memory on any book's main message before reading
3. **Study Planning** - Identify which books to study based on themes that interest you
4. **Big Picture** - See how individual books fit into the larger biblical narrative
5. **Application** - Find immediate ways to apply biblical truth to daily life
