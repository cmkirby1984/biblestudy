# Old Testament (39 Books)

The Old Testament tells the story of God's relationship with humanity from creation through the history of Israel up to approximately 400 BC.

---

## The Law (Pentateuch) - 5 Books
The foundation of Jewish law and the covenant relationship between God and Israel.

- [Genesis]({{ '/old-testament/01-genesis/' | relative_url }}) - Beginnings: creation, fall, patriarchs, and promises
- [Exodus]({{ '/old-testament/02-exodus/' | relative_url }}) - Liberation from Egypt and receiving the Law at Sinai
- [Leviticus]({{ '/old-testament/03-leviticus/' | relative_url }}) - Laws for holiness, worship, and priestly duties
- [Numbers]({{ '/old-testament/04-numbers/' | relative_url }}) - Wilderness wanderings and preparation to enter Canaan
- [Deuteronomy]({{ '/old-testament/05-deuteronomy/' | relative_url }}) - Moses' final words and covenant renewal

---

## Historical Books - 12 Books
The history of Israel from entering the Promised Land through exile and return.

- [Joshua]({{ '/old-testament/06-joshua/' | relative_url }}) - Conquering and settling the Promised Land
- [Judges]({{ '/old-testament/07-judges/' | relative_url }}) - Cycles of sin and deliverance before the monarchy
- [Ruth]({{ '/old-testament/08-ruth/' | relative_url }}) - Loyalty and redemption in the lineage of David
- [1 Samuel]({{ '/old-testament/09-1-samuel/' | relative_url }}) - Transition from judges to kingship: Samuel, Saul, David
- [2 Samuel]({{ '/old-testament/10-2-samuel/' | relative_url }}) - David's reign and God's covenant with David
- [1 Kings]({{ '/old-testament/11-1-kings/' | relative_url }}) - Solomon's wisdom and the kingdom divides
- [2 Kings]({{ '/old-testament/12-2-kings/' | relative_url }}) - Decline and fall of both kingdoms into exile
- [1 Chronicles]({{ '/old-testament/13-1-chronicles/' | relative_url }}) - David's reign from a priestly perspective
- [2 Chronicles]({{ '/old-testament/14-2-chronicles/' | relative_url }}) - Solomon through Judah's exile
- [Ezra]({{ '/old-testament/15-ezra/' | relative_url }}) - Return from exile and rebuilding the temple
- [Nehemiah]({{ '/old-testament/16-nehemiah/' | relative_url }}) - Rebuilding Jerusalem's walls and spiritual renewal
- [Esther]({{ '/old-testament/17-esther/' | relative_url }}) - God's providence and deliverance of the Jews in Persia

---

## Wisdom Literature - 5 Books
Poetry, wisdom, and worship literature exploring life's deep questions.

- [Job]({{ '/old-testament/18-job/' | relative_url }}) - Suffering, sovereignty, and trusting God
- [Psalms]({{ '/old-testament/19-psalms/' | relative_url }}) - 150 songs of worship, lament, and praise
- [Proverbs]({{ '/old-testament/20-proverbs/' | relative_url }}) - Practical wisdom for godly living
- [Ecclesiastes]({{ '/old-testament/21-ecclesiastes/' | relative_url }}) - Life's meaning and purpose under the sun
- [Song of Solomon]({{ '/old-testament/22-song-of-solomon/' | relative_url }}) - Celebration of love and marriage

---

## Major Prophets - 5 Books
Lengthy prophetic messages calling for repentance and promising hope.

- [Isaiah]({{ '/old-testament/23-isaiah/' | relative_url }}) - Judgment, salvation, and the coming Messiah
- [Jeremiah]({{ '/old-testament/24-jeremiah/' | relative_url }}) - Warnings of judgment and promises of a new covenant
- [Lamentations]({{ '/old-testament/25-lamentations/' | relative_url }}) - Mourning Jerusalem's destruction
- [Ezekiel]({{ '/old-testament/26-ezekiel/' | relative_url }}) - Visions of God's glory, judgment, and restoration
- [Daniel]({{ '/old-testament/27-daniel/' | relative_url }}) - Faithfulness in exile and prophetic visions

---

## Minor Prophets - 12 Books
Shorter but powerful prophetic books spanning 400 years.

- [Hosea]({{ '/old-testament/28-hosea/' | relative_url }}) - God's faithful love despite unfaithfulness
- [Joel]({{ '/old-testament/29-joel/' | relative_url }}) - The Day of the Lord and outpouring of the Spirit
- [Amos]({{ '/old-testament/30-amos/' | relative_url }}) - Justice, righteousness, and social concern
- [Obadiah]({{ '/old-testament/31-obadiah/' | relative_url }}) - Judgment on Edom for pride and violence
- [Jonah]({{ '/old-testament/32-jonah/' | relative_url }}) - God's mercy extends to all nations
- [Micah]({{ '/old-testament/33-micah/' | relative_url }}) - Justice, mercy, and walking humbly with God
- [Nahum]({{ '/old-testament/34-nahum/' | relative_url }}) - God's judgment on Nineveh
- [Habakkuk]({{ '/old-testament/35-habakkuk/' | relative_url }}) - Wrestling with God's justice and trusting in faith
- [Zephaniah]({{ '/old-testament/36-zephaniah/' | relative_url }}) - The Day of the Lord and call to seek God
- [Haggai]({{ '/old-testament/37-haggai/' | relative_url }}) - Prioritizing God's house and work
- [Zechariah]({{ '/old-testament/38-zechariah/' | relative_url }}) - Visions of restoration and the coming King
- [Malachi]({{ '/old-testament/39-malachi/' | relative_url }}) - Call to faithfulness and promise of Elijah's return
