# Ezekiel

## Book Overview

**Author:** Ezekiel, priest and prophet
**Date of Writing:** Approximately 593-570 BC
**Historical Context:** Babylonian exile; prophesies before and after Jerusalem's fall (586 BC)
**Original Audience:** Jewish exiles in Babylon
**Purpose:** To explain reasons for exile, pronounce judgment on nations, and promise future restoration with new temple
**Genre:** Prophetic oracle with visions, symbolic actions, poetry, and prose

## Key Themes & Messages

### Major Themes

1. **God's Glory and Sovereignty**
   - Inaugural vision of God's throne-chariot demonstrates divine majesty
   - Glory departs from corrupted temple before destruction
   - Glory will return to purified temple in restoration
   - Repeated phrase: "Then they will know that I am the LORD"

2. **Individual Responsibility**
   - "The soul who sins shall die" - personal accountability
   - Cannot blame previous generations
   - Each person responsible for their own choices
   - Opportunity for repentance and life

3. **Judgment on Israel and Nations**
   - Comprehensive indictment of Judah's sins
   - Idolatry, injustice, and covenant unfaithfulness
   - Surrounding nations also judged for their sins
   - God's holiness requires dealing with sin

4. **Restoration and New Life**
   - Vision of dry bones: national resurrection and return
   - New heart and new Spirit promised
   - Davidic king will shepherd united people
   - God will dwell with His people forever

5. **The New Temple and God's Presence**
   - Detailed vision of restored temple (chapters 40-48)
   - River of life flowing from temple brings healing
   - God's presence permanently dwelling with purified people
   - Ultimate vision of perfect worship and fellowship

### Central Message

Ezekiel emphasizes God's holiness, which demands judgment on persistent sin, while promising future restoration through heart transformation. God's glory departed from the corrupted temple but will return to dwell with a renewed people. Through the Spirit's work, God will give new hearts, raise the dead nation to life, and establish His eternal presence among His people in a purified new creation.
