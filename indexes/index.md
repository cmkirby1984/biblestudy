# Study Indexes

Comprehensive indexes to help you explore themes, topics, and people throughout the Bible.

---

## 📚 [Thematic Index]({{ '/indexes/thematic-index/' | relative_url }})

A comprehensive topical index showing where major themes appear throughout Scripture. Perfect for topical studies that span multiple books.

**Features:**
- 80+ major biblical themes organized alphabetically
- References showing where each theme appears in the Bible
- Brief descriptions of how themes develop across Scripture
- Cross-references between related topics

**Popular themes include:**
- Faith, Grace, Love, Salvation, Redemption
- Prayer, Worship, Covenant, Kingdom
- Sin, Repentance, Forgiveness, Justice
- Hope, Peace, Joy, Patience
- Leadership, Obedience, Wisdom

**Perfect for:**
- Preparing topical sermon series
- Personal devotional studies on specific themes
- Small group studies focused on biblical topics
- Research on how themes develop from Old to New Testament

---

## 👥 [Character Index]({{ '/indexes/character-index/' | relative_url }})

A guide to 39 major biblical figures, their stories, and the lessons we can learn from their lives.

**Features:**
- Detailed profiles of key Bible characters
- Major events and life lessons from each person
- Character strengths and weaknesses
- Relevant Scripture references
- Practical applications from their lives

**Characters include:**
- **Patriarchs:** Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph
- **Leaders:** Moses, Joshua, Samuel, David, Solomon
- **Prophets:** Elijah, Elisha, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Daniel
- **Kings:** Saul, David, Solomon, Hezekiah, Josiah
- **New Testament:** John the Baptist, Peter, Paul, Mary, Mary Magdalene
- **Women:** Sarah, Ruth, Esther, Hannah, Deborah

**Perfect for:**
- Character study sermon series
- Learning from biblical examples
- Understanding leadership principles
- Biographical Bible studies
- Teaching on faith heroes

## How to Use These Indexes

### For Thematic Studies:
1. Choose a theme that interests you
2. Look up the theme in the Thematic Index
3. Read the referenced passages in order
4. Note how the theme develops throughout Scripture
5. Apply what you learn to your life

### For Character Studies:
1. Select a biblical character to study
2. Read their profile in the Character Index
3. Look up the key passages mentioned
4. Note their strengths, weaknesses, and growth
5. Identify practical lessons for your own life
