## 🗺️ [Maps & Geography]({{ '/resources/maps' | relative_url }})

Understanding the geography of Bible lands enriches your study by providing context for events, journeys, and narratives.

**What's Included:**
- Key biblical locations and their significance
- Important distances and travel times in Bible times
- Major geographical regions (Israel, Judah, surrounding nations)
- Notable journeys (Abraham's travels, Exodus route, Paul's missionary journeys)
- Recommended online map resources
- Downloadable map collections

**Biblical Regions Covered:**
- The Promised Land (Canaan/Israel)
- Egypt and the Exodus route
- Mesopotamia (Babylon, Assyria, Persia)
- The Mediterranean world (Greece, Rome)
- Asia Minor (Turkey region)
- First-century Palestine

**Perfect for:**
- Visualizing biblical narratives
- Understanding travel distances and time periods
- Teaching geography in Bible studies
- Following missionary journeys
- Understanding military campaigns and conquests
