# Biblical Timeline

A chronological overview of Bible history, showing when books were written and the major periods they cover.

---

## Quick Overview

| Period | Dates (Approximate) | Key Events | Books |
|--------|---------------------|------------|-------|
| **Creation-Flood** | Unknown-2350 BC | Creation, Fall, Flood | Genesis 1-11 |
| **Patriarchs** | 2000-1800 BC | Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph | Genesis 12-50, Job |
| **Egypt & Exodus** | 1800-1445 BC | Israel in Egypt, Exodus | Exodus |
| **Wilderness** | 1445-1405 BC | Mt. Sinai, 40 years wandering | Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy |
| **Conquest** | 1405-1385 BC | Joshua conquers Canaan | Joshua |
| **Judges** | 1385-1050 BC | Cycles of sin and deliverance | Judges, Ruth |
| **United Kingdom** | 1050-930 BC | Saul, David, Solomon | 1 Samuel - 1 Kings 11, many Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Solomon |
| **Divided Kingdom** | 930-722 BC (Israel)<br>930-586 BC (Judah) | Two kingdoms, prophets warn | 1 Kings 12 - 2 Kings 17 (Israel)<br>1 Kings 12 - 2 Kings 25 (Judah)<br>Obadiah, Joel, Jonah, Amos, Hosea, Isaiah, Micah, Nahum, Zephaniah, Jeremiah, Habakkuk |
| **Exile** | 586-539 BC | Judah in Babylon | Lamentations, Ezekiel, Daniel |
| **Return** | 539-430 BC | Rebuild temple, walls, community | Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi |
| **Intertestamental** | 430-5 BC | 400 years of silence | (No biblical books) |
| **Life of Christ** | 5 BC - AD 30 | Birth, ministry, death, resurrection | Matthew, Mark, Luke, John |
| **Early Church** | AD 30-100 | Church begins, spreads to Rome | Acts, all epistles, Revelation |

## Detailed Timeline

### PRIMEVAL HISTORY (Genesis 1-11)
**Dates:** Unknown - c. 2000 BC
**Key Events:** Creation, Fall, Cain and Abel, Flood, Tower of Babel

**Books Written:**
- *Genesis* (written c. 1445-1405 BC by Moses, covering this period)

---

### PATRIARCHAL PERIOD (2000-1800 BC)
**Key Figures:** Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph
**Key Events:**
- Abraham called from Ur (c. 2000 BC)
- Isaac born (c. 1900 BC)
- Jacob and Esau born (c. 1850 BC)
- Joseph in Egypt (c. 1890-1800 BC)

**Books Written:**
- *Genesis 12-50* (written c. 1445-1405 BC, covers this period)
- *Job* (events likely during this period; date of writing uncertain)

---

### EGYPT & THE EXODUS (1800-1445 BC)
**Key Events:**
- Israel multiplies in Egypt (1800-1445 BC)
- Moses born (c. 1525 BC)
- The Exodus from Egypt (c. 1445 BC)
- Crossing the Red Sea
- Ten Commandments at Sinai

**Books Written:**
- *Exodus* (written c. 1445-1405 BC by Moses)

---

### WILDERNESS WANDERINGS (1445-1405 BC)
**Key Events:**
- Law given at Mount Sinai
- Golden calf incident
- Tabernacle constructed
- Spies sent to Canaan
- 40 years of wandering
- New generation prepared

**Books Written (all by Moses):**
- *Leviticus* (1445-1405 BC)
- *Numbers* (1445-1405 BC)
- *Deuteronomy* (c. 1405 BC)
- *Psalm 90* (attributed to Moses)

---

### CONQUEST OF CANAAN (1405-1385 BC)
**Key Leader:** Joshua
**Key Events:**
- Crossing Jordan River
- Fall of Jericho
- Conquest campaigns
- Land divided among tribes
- Covenant renewal at Shechem

**Books Written:**
- *Joshua* (c. 1405-1385 BC)

---

### PERIOD OF THE JUDGES (1385-1050 BC)
**Key Events:**
- Repeated cycles: sin, oppression, deliverance
- Judges: Deborah, Gideon, Samson, etc.
- "Everyone did what was right in his own eyes"

**Books Written:**
- *Judges* (c. 1045-1000 BC)
- *Ruth* (c. 1000 BC, set during Judges period)

---

### UNITED MONARCHY (1050-930 BC)

#### Under Saul (1050-1010 BC)
- First king of Israel
- Tragic decline

#### Under David (1010-970 BC)
- United kingdom
- Jerusalem as capital
- Covenant with David (2 Sam 7)
- Military victories

#### Under Solomon (970-930 BC)
- Temple built (966-959 BC)
- Wisdom and prosperity
- Decline into idolatry

**Books Written:**
- *1 Samuel* (c. 930 BC, covering 1100-1010 BC)
- *2 Samuel* (c. 930 BC, covering 1010-970 BC)
- *1 Kings 1-11* (c. 560-540 BC, covering 970-930 BC)
- *1 Chronicles* (c. 450-400 BC, retelling history)
- Many *Psalms* by David and others
- *Proverbs* (primarily Solomon, c. 970-930 BC, compiled later)
- *Ecclesiastes* (traditionally Solomon, c. 935 BC)
- *Song of Solomon* (traditionally Solomon, c. 970-930 BC)

---

### DIVIDED KINGDOM (930-722/586 BC)

#### Northern Kingdom - Israel (930-722 BC)
**Capital:** Samaria
**Kings:** All evil (Jeroboam through Hoshea)
**End:** Assyrian conquest (722 BC)

#### Southern Kingdom - Judah (930-586 BC)
**Capital:** Jerusalem
**Kings:** Mixed record (Rehoboam through Zedekiah)
**End:** Babylonian conquest (586 BC)

**Key Events:**
- Division after Solomon (930 BC)
- Elijah vs. Baal prophets (c. 860 BC)
- Jonah to Nineveh (c. 760 BC)
- Fall of Israel to Assyria (722 BC)
- Hezekiah's reforms (715-686 BC)
- Josiah's reforms (640-609 BC)
- First Babylonian deportation (605 BC - Daniel taken)
- Second deportation (597 BC - Ezekiel taken)
- Jerusalem destroyed (586 BC)

**Books Written:**
- *1 Kings 12-22* and *2 Kings* (c. 560-540 BC)
- *2 Chronicles* (c. 450-400 BC, retelling)

**Prophets to Israel (Northern Kingdom):**
- *Jonah* (c. 760 BC)
- *Amos* (c. 760 BC)
- *Hosea* (c. 755-715 BC)

**Prophets to Judah (Southern Kingdom):**
- *Obadiah* (c. 845 BC or 585 BC - disputed)
- *Joel* (c. 835 BC or 400 BC - disputed)
- *Isaiah* (c. 740-680 BC)
- *Micah* (c. 735-710 BC)
- *Nahum* (c. 650 BC)
- *Zephaniah* (c. 635-625 BC)
- *Jeremiah* (c. 627-585 BC)
- *Habakkuk* (c. 609-605 BC)

---

### BABYLONIAN EXILE (586-539 BC)
**Key Events:**
- Judah in Babylon (586-539 BC)
- Daniel and friends in royal court
- Ezekiel among exiles
- Jerusalem and temple destroyed

**Books Written:**
- *Lamentations* (c. 586 BC by Jeremiah)
- *Ezekiel* (c. 593-570 BC)
- *Daniel* (c. 530 BC, covering 605-530 BC events)

---

### POST-EXILIC PERIOD (539-430 BC)
**Key Events:**
- Cyrus of Persia conquers Babylon (539 BC)
- First return under Zerubbabel (538 BC)
- Temple rebuilt (520-516 BC)
- Second return under Ezra (458 BC)
- Third return under Nehemiah (444 BC)
- Walls rebuilt (444 BC)

**Books Written:**
- *Ezra* (c. 450-440 BC, covering 538-458 BC)
- *Nehemiah* (c. 430-420 BC, covering 444-430 BC)
- *Esther* (c. 470-350 BC, events 483-473 BC)
- *Haggai* (520 BC)
- *Zechariah* (520-480 BC)
- *Malachi* (c. 450-430 BC)

---

### INTERTESTAMENTAL PERIOD (430 BC - 5 BC)
**"400 Years of Silence"**
**Key Events:**
- Persian rule continues (539-332 BC)
- Alexander the Great conquers (332 BC)
- Greek rule and Hellenization (332-167 BC)
- Maccabean revolt (167-63 BC)
- Roman rule begins (63 BC)
- Herod the Great (37-4 BC)

**Books Written:** None (biblical canon closed)

---

### LIFE AND MINISTRY OF JESUS (5 BC - AD 30)
**Key Events:**
- Birth of Jesus (c. 5-4 BC)
- Ministry begins (c. AD 27)
- Crucifixion and resurrection (c. AD 30)
- Ascension (AD 30)

**Books Written (AD 50-90):**
- *Matthew* (c. 50-70)
- *Mark* (c. 55-65)
- *Luke* (c. 60-65)
- *John* (c. 85-90)

---

### APOSTOLIC AGE (AD 30-100)
**Key Events:**
- Pentecost (AD 30)
- Stephen martyred (AD 34)
- Paul's conversion (AD 35)
- Paul's missionary journeys (AD 46-58)
- Jerusalem council (AD 49)
- Paul imprisoned in Rome (AD 60-62)
- Temple destroyed (AD 70)
- John exiled to Patmos (c. AD 95)

**Books Written:**

**Early Epistles:**
- *James* (c. 45-50)
- *Galatians* (c. 49)

**Paul's Letters (1st and 2nd Missionary Journeys):**
- *1 Thessalonians* (c. 50-51)
- *2 Thessalonians* (c. 50-51)

**Paul's Letters (3rd Missionary Journey):**
- *1 Corinthians* (c. 55)
- *2 Corinthians* (c. 56)
- *Romans* (c. 57)

**Paul's Prison Epistles:**
- *Ephesians* (c. 60-62)
- *Philippians* (c. 60-62)
- *Colossians* (c. 60-62)
- *Philemon* (c. 60-62)

**Paul's Pastoral Epistles:**
- *1 Timothy* (c. 63)
- *Titus* (c. 63)
- *2 Timothy* (c. 67)

**General Epistles:**
- *1 Peter* (c. 63-64)
- *2 Peter* (c. 65-67)
- *Jude* (c. 65-80)
- *1 John* (c. 85-90)
- *2 John* (c. 85-90)
- *3 John* (c. 85-90)

**Historical:**
- *Acts* (c. 62-65)

**Prophetic:**
- *Hebrews* (c. 65-70)
- *Revelation* (c. 95)

---

## Timeline Summary by Book (Chronological Order of Events Covered)

1. **Genesis** - Creation to Joseph in Egypt
2. **Job** - Patriarchal period (possibly)
3. **Exodus** - Slavery to Mt. Sinai
4. **Leviticus** - Laws at Sinai
5. **Numbers** - Wilderness wanderings
6. **Deuteronomy** - End of wanderings, Moses' sermons
7. **Joshua** - Conquest of Canaan
8. **Judges** - Cycles of sin and deliverance
9. **Ruth** - During Judges period
10. **1 Samuel** - Samuel, Saul, young David
11. **2 Samuel** - David's reign
12. **1 Kings 1-11** - Solomon's reign
13. **1 Kings 12 - 2 Kings 17** - Divided kingdom to fall of Israel
14. **2 Kings 18-25** - Judah alone to exile
15. **1 Chronicles** - Creation to David (retelling)
16. **2 Chronicles** - Solomon to exile (retelling)
17. **Ezra** - Return from exile, temple rebuilt
18. **Nehemiah** - Walls rebuilt
19. **Esther** - Jews in Persia
20. **Prophets** - (See dates above)
21. **Wisdom Literature** - Various dates
22. **Gospels** - Life of Jesus
23. **Acts** - Early church
24. **Epistles** - Teaching to early churches
25. **Revelation** - Vision of end times

---

## Notes on Dating

- Dates for Old Testament events and writings are approximate and sometimes debated among scholars
- Some books (Job, Ecclesiastes, Psalms) have uncertain dates of composition
- New Testament dating is more certain but still has some variation among scholars
- This guide uses traditional conservative evangelical dating
